# CareTask Status Lifecycle

## Statuses

### Planned

The CareTask is part of Today's CareTaskList but has not yet been assigned to a Caregiver.

### Assigned

The CareTask has been assigned to a Caregiver.

### Completed

The assigned Caregiver completed the CareTask.

### UnableToComplete

The Caregiver could not complete the CareTask and provided a reason.

### NeedAssistance

The Caregiver needs help to complete the CareTask.

### Overdue

The due time has passed and the CareTask is not completed.

### Missed

The CareTask remained incomplete after the assigned Caregiver’s shift ended.

### Cancelled

The CareTask was cancelled by an authorized CareCoordinator.
